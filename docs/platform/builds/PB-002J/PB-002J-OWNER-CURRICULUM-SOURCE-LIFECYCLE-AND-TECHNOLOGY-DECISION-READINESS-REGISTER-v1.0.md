## 3. Approved Constraints — Not Open for Reconsideration

The following approved PB-002J and PI-001 constraints remain controlling. This register records them as locked inputs; it does not reopen, replace, weaken, or treat them as unresolved decisions:

- Effective per-student availability is the class baseline plus approved individual additions minus approved individual removals.
- A student may receive no more than three verified primary Start Mission choices after class settings and individual overrides are resolved.
- A Goal is an organizer by default and does not consume a primary Start position unless an authoritative catalog record separately verifies it as an independently distributable Mission.
- Verified Continue work remains separate, protected, and Continue-first. Removing Start availability does not delete or hide verified Continue work.
- Side Paths remain secondary choices and may not displace, imitate, or bypass the three primary Mission Start positions.
- One class-wide current classroom focus remains controlling. No personalized or inferred replacement focus may be created for an individual student.
- Selected-student Mission distribution permits no more than one active individual distribution relationship per student, classroom, Mission, and Mission version. Idempotent and concurrent operations must preserve that uniqueness rule.
- Cross-grade selection remains proposed and unauthorized. Confirmation, persistence, and projection must fail closed unless and until the required product, curriculum/safety, school-operations, and accessibility decisions are separately approved.
- PB-002I remains a protected, session-only Today’s Mission communication boundary and is not an authoritative PB-002J assignment, availability, catalog, or projection source.

These constraints are mandatory inputs to every owner, lifecycle, privacy, technology, repository, and implementation decision below.
